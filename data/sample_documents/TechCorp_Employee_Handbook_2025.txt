Section 1: Working Hours and Remote Work Policy
Full-time employees are expected to work 40 hours per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Every remote employee is eligible for an annual home-office stipend of ,200 for ergonomics and high-speed internet.

Section 2: Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
New hires accrue 18 days of paid time off during their first year of employment.
After completing three years of continuous service, the annual PTO entitlement increases to 25 days.
Unused PTO can be rolled over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year.

Section 3: Health and Wellness Benefits
TechCorp covers 90% of health insurance premiums for employees and 75% for eligible dependents.
Wellness benefits include a  monthly gym or fitness reimbursement.
Mental health support includes 10 free confidential therapy sessions per year through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

Section 4: Parental Leave
All primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child.
Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave.
Notice must be submitted to Human Resources at least 30 days prior to the expected commencement date.
